Privacy and limitations
This client uses the free Mail.tm service and its API. The provider controls availability, supported domains, retention and filtering. Do not use temporary inboxes for banking, password recovery, or important accounts. Your inbox credentials are saved in this browser's local storage so you can reopen the inbox on this device. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access it. This app receives email only; it does not send email.
